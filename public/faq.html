Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about the SIDHI GYM personal workout and running tracker.

What is SIDHI GYM?

SIDHI GYM is a web-based personal fitness tracker for logging gym workouts, running or walking activity, body weight and progress.

Is SIDHI GYM a physical gym?

No. SIDHI GYM Tracker is software for personal fitness logging, not a physical gym.

Can I track running with GPS?

Yes, the app has a separate Run area with GPS tracking where supported. You must grant location permission, and GPS quality depends on your device and browser.

Can I track body weight and workout history?

Yes. Users can record workout and weight entries and review available calendar, graph and history views.

Can I install SIDHI GYM on my phone?

Supported browsers may allow SIDHI GYM to be added to the home screen as a web app. Availability depends on your device and browser.

Does SIDHI GYM provide medical advice?

No. It is a fitness logging tool and does not replace medical advice or professional coaching.

How is my information handled?

Read the Privacy Policy for the app's data collection and handling information. Contact the developer through the Contact page for questions.
