ABOUT THE APP

Fitness progress, recorded consistently.

SIDHI GYM is a personal gym and running tracker that helps you keep your workout history, body-weight records and fitness progress in one place.

What is SIDHI GYM?

SIDHI GYM Tracker is a web-based fitness log created to make everyday workout tracking straightforward. Users can create an account, record gym sessions, track running or walking activity, review weight trends and look back at their training history.

It is a digital tracking tool, not a physical gym, medical service or substitute for advice from a qualified fitness or healthcare professional.

Built for consistent training

The app focuses on practical records: what training you completed, when you trained, the weights you recorded, and how your activity changes over time. A progress calendar and history help you review your own consistency rather than relying on memory.

Who is it for?

People who want a simple personal log for gym workouts, running, walking, body-weight changes and fitness goals. You can use the website in a mobile or desktop browser. On supported browsers, it can also be added to your home screen as a progressive web app.

Privacy and account control

Workout records are associated with your account. Read the Privacy Policy to understand the data the app describes collecting, and review the Terms before using the service.
