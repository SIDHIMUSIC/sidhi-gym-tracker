FEATURES

Your training log, all in one place.

SIDHI GYM brings everyday gym records, running activity and body-weight progress together in a personal fitness tracker.

Gym workout tracking

Record workout dates, training notes, exercise details, entry and finish times, and session completion. Use your workout history to review previous training sessions.

Body-weight and goal tracking

Save weight measurements, set a target weight and review changes using weight graphs and calendar history. Measurements are user-entered records, not medical assessments.

Running and walking log

Use the separate Run area to record outdoor running or walking activity with GPS where your device and browser support location access. Location permission and a suitable GPS signal are required for GPS tracking.

Progress calendar and streaks

Review recorded training days in the calendar and monitor your gym consistency. Rest days are treated separately from missed workout days according to the app's schedule logic.

Workout history and reports

Open past session records, review logged weights and training details, and use available export or sharing options in the app. Availability of some advanced tools may depend on account or premium configuration.

Installable web app

SIDHI GYM includes a web app manifest and service worker. Supported browsers may offer an Add to Home Screen or Install option. Installation behavior varies by browser and device.
