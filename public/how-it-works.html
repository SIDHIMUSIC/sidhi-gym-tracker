GETTING STARTED

How to use SIDHI GYM

Start with an account, add your training records and use the progress views to understand your consistency.

1. Create your account

Open the app and choose Create Account. Follow the on-screen instructions, use an email address you can access, and keep your sign-in details secure.

2. Log a gym workout

Open the Workout tab, choose the date and record your gym entry, body weight, exercises, treadmill details and finish weight as applicable. Save your entry, then use Finish Workout when the session is complete.

3. Record a run or walk

Open the Run tab and start a GPS activity. Allow location access when prompted. GPS accuracy and background tracking depend on your phone, browser permissions, battery settings and network conditions.

4. Review your progress

Use the Home dashboard, Progress calendar, weight graph and History to review saved activity. Keep entries accurate and remember that short-term body-weight changes are normal.

5. Install on your phone (optional)

In a supported browser, open the browser menu and look for Install App or Add to Home Screen. The exact wording varies by device.
