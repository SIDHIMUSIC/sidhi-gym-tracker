Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Sidhi Gym Tracker (“we”, “the app”) is a free gym and run log at sidhi-gym-tracker.vercel.app and sidhigym.dpdns.org. This page explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

Account data you enter: name, username, password (stored hashed), email, gender. Workout data you log: weight, dates, times, exercises, runs, water, protein, height, and goal weight. Technical data: browser type, device, and pages visited, used to keep the app working.

How we use it

To show your dashboard, save workouts, send password reset codes, and improve the app. We do not sell your personal data.

Ads

We use Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized ads at adssettings.google.com.

Cookies

We use a login token in local storage so you stay signed in. AdSense and analytics may set cookies. You can block cookies in your browser; some features may stop working.

Sharing

Data is stored on our servers (MongoDB via Vercel). We share data only with services needed to run the app (hosting, email for OTP, Google for ads) or if required by law.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, export your data as JSON or CSV, or delete your account from the Profile section. Deleting removes your account and workout records from our database.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13. Do not create an account if you are under 13.
